POKEDEX
UI KIT

The 8-bit design system behind the Ashoka Pokedex — a dex of real people built by and for a university campus. One CSS file, no build step, no dependencies, no framework. Write plain HTML and it looks like a 1996 handheld.

02 — THE ONE RULE

No blur. Ever. No border-radius, no soft shadows, no gradients on small elements, no antialiased text. Depth comes from hard 4px offsets in flat black. The moment something blurs it stops reading as a handheld and starts reading as a web app. Every other decision here follows from that one.

RIGHT — HARD EDGES
4px offset, flat black
WRONG — BLURRED
rounded + soft shadow

03 — TOKENS

Override these in your own :root and the entire kit recolours. Nothing is hardcoded downstream.

--red
--red-dark
--screen
--screen-2
--ink
--ink-dim
--amber
--cyan
--lime
--danger

--px: 4px is the offset every frame is built from, and --edge: #05080c is the flat black it is drawn in. Change --px to 6px and the whole UI gets chunkier.

10 — BUILD YOUR OWN DEX

The kit is just the look. The whole application behind it — access codes, accounts, photo capture that crushes to a dithered sprite in the browser, a two-admin approval queue, and rarity that is earned from sightings rather than assigned — is open source and built to be forked.

github.com/nanj-dnd/ashoka-pokedex

Next.js + Supabase, deploys to Vercel. Swap the campus vocabulary in src/lib/constants.ts — types, habitats, stats, rarity thresholds — and it is your institution's dex, not ours.
